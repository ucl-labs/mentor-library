## Key takeaways

- **List vs verdict.** Google handed over ten blue links and let the buyer compare. AI reads the
  options for them, weighs the sources, and returns a conclusion naming two or three businesses.
  Everyone else is excluded and the buyer never knows you were an option.
- **AI-referred traffic converts ~8x better** than traditional traffic, and faster, because the
  visitor arrives pre-vetted and pre-sold. Rankings dashboards cannot see it.
- **Perplexity now shows its trust map** with four source tiers and states why each was earned.
  ChatGPT and Gemini make the same judgment without printing the badge.
- **82-factor study:** relevancy ranked first, **brand mentions ranked second** (nearly tied).
  Then reviews, then authority.
- **Across six AI platforms, third-party citations scored high on every one.** Expert authorship
  scored 4+/5 on every platform. **Backlinks scored 1.9/5 on ChatGPT.**
- **Compression is the risk.** AI compresses everything published about you into one paragraph.
  The machine does not fill gaps. Muddy signals in, muddy paragraph out.
- **The org chart is the vulnerability.** Website, ads, social, PR, and reviews run as separate
  functions each tell a slightly different story. The machine reads all of it as one document.
- **Do not respond by publishing more.** You cannot out-publish this. More of a contradictory story
  just makes the contradiction louder. The play is fewer, stronger signals.

## Slide captured

**The six-pillar reputation system** (slide, verbatim):

1. Content that establishes what we believe.
2. A website that says exactly who we are.
3. Real names attached to real expertise.
4. Case studies that document what we've done.
5. Reviews that prove the experience.
6. Original research that gives other people a reason to cite us.
