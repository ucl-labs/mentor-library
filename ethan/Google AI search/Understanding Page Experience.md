# Understanding Page Experience in Google Search Results

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience

Google's core ranking systems reward content that provides excellent page experience. Rather than focusing on isolated aspects, site owners should strive for "an overall great page experience across many aspects."

## Self-Assessment Questions

Evaluate your site by answering yes to these questions:

- Do your pages have good Core Web Vitals?
- Are pages served securely?
- Does content display well on mobile devices?
- Does your site avoid excessive ads that distract from main content?
- Do pages avoid intrusive interstitials?
- Can visitors easily distinguish main content from other page elements?

## FAQ Highlights

**On ranking signals:** There is no single "page experience signal." Core ranking systems evaluate multiple signals, and "Core Web Vitals are used by our ranking systems."

**Beyond Core Web Vitals:** While other page experience aspects don't directly boost rankings, they improve user satisfaction, which aligns with what ranking systems reward.

**Evaluation scope:** Core ranking systems generally assess content on a page-specific basis, though some site-wide assessments exist.

**Importance:** Google prioritizes relevance above all, but "having a great page experience can contribute to success in Search" when multiple helpful content options exist.
