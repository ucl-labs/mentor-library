# Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

## Overview

Google's official guide emphasizes that SEO best practices remain foundational for generative AI search optimization. The document clarifies that "the best practices for SEO continue to be relevant because our generative AI features on Google Search are rooted in our core Search ranking and quality systems."

## Key Optimization Areas

**Content Strategy**
Google recommends focusing on unique, valuable content that provides distinctive perspectives. Rather than creating commodity content, websites should offer "first-hand review[s]" or expert insights that "go beyond common knowledge and the ordinary."

**Technical Foundation**
Pages must meet standard search requirements and be indexable. The guide advises ensuring content is "crawlable, as Google Search generative AI models use publicly accessible, crawlable content."

**Visual Elements**
Including "high-quality, relevant images and videos on your pages" can expand visibility beyond text links in AI responses.

## Debunking Misconceptions

Google explicitly states you don't need:
- Special files like llms.txt
- Content "chunking" into tiny pieces
- AI-specific writing styles
- Inauthentic mentions across the web

## Practical Next Steps

Website owners should prioritize established SEO fundamentals over emerging "AEO" or "GEO" tactics, maintain clear site structure, and create people-first content. The guide stresses that optimization efforts should serve visitors first, as effective user satisfaction drives visibility in both traditional and AI-enhanced search results.
