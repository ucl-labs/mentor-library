# Creating Helpful, Reliable, People-First Content

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

Google's automated ranking systems prioritize helpful, reliable information created to benefit people rather than manipulate search rankings.

## Self-Assessment Framework

The page provides evaluation questions across two main categories:

**Content Quality**: Does your work offer original research, comprehensive coverage, and substantial value? Does it avoid spelling errors and appear professionally produced? Would you bookmark or recommend it?

**Expertise**: Is authorship clear with verifiable credentials? Would researchers recognize the site as trustworthy? Are there factual errors?

## Key Concepts

**E-E-A-T**: Google's systems identify Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness - with trust being most critical. This applies especially to "Your Money or Your Life" topics affecting health, finances, or safety.

**People-First vs. Search Engine-First**: The guide warns against creating content "primarily made to attract visits from search engines" or using "extensive automation to produce content on many topics."

**The "Who, How, Why" Framework**:
- **Who**: Clear authorship with bylines
- **How**: Transparent process descriptions, including AI/automation disclosure
- **Why**: Content created primarily to help people, not manipulate rankings

## Automation Guidelines

When using AI or automation, consider whether disclosures about these tools would be "reasonably expected" by readers.
