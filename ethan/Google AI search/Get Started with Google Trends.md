# Get started with Google Trends

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/trends-start

Google Trends is a tool that helps content creators understand search behavior patterns. It "provides a random sample of aggregated, anonymized, and categorized Google and YouTube searches," allowing analysis of query interest globally or at city levels.

## Main Tools

1. **Explore tool** - for analyzing custom terms, regional interest, and topic evolution over time
2. **Trending now tool** - for discovering current trending topics with approximate search volume and related news articles

## Key Use Cases

**Monitoring Rising Trends**: Evaluate whether trending topics align with your industry and audience before publishing content about them.

**Keyword Research**: Identify search terms your audience uses by adding up to 5 terms to the Explore tool, reviewing related topics, and checking geographic data. Cross-reference findings with Search Console Performance reports.

**Content Calendar Planning**: By identifying seasonal patterns, publish content before peak search periods. "Trends may differ across countries," making region-specific analysis important.

**Industry Benchmarking**: Comparing search interest trends helps distinguish between industry-wide fluctuations and site-specific traffic changes.

**Brand Monitoring**: Searching your business name reveals what terms people associate with your brand.

## Important Note

Only write about trending topics if they're relevant to your business and audience, referencing the guide on "creating helpful, reliable, people-first content."
