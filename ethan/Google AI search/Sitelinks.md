# Sitelinks

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/sitelinks

**Sitelinks** are supplementary links displayed beneath a main search result, all originating from the same domain. "Our systems analyze the link structure of your site to find shortcuts that will save users time and allow them to quickly find the information they're looking for."

## When Sitelinks Appear

Google only displays sitelinks when they provide genuine user value. "If the structure of your site doesn't allow our algorithms to find good sitelinks, or we don't think that the sitelinks for your site are relevant for the user's query, we won't show them."

## Best Practices for Sitelinks

- Use informative, relevant, and concise page titles and heading text
- Establish a logical, user-friendly site structure with prominent internal linking to important pages
- Ensure internal anchor text is descriptive and directly related to destination pages
- Eliminate content duplication

## Removing Sitelinks

For unwanted sitelinks, Google suggests either removing the page entirely or applying the `noindex` directive to prevent its display in search results.

Note: Sitelink generation remains an automated process. Direct control options remain limited.
