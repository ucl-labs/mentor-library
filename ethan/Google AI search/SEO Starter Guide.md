# Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Starter Guide

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide

## Core Message

SEO is about helping search engines understand your content and making your site discoverable. Following Search Essentials makes sites more likely to appear in results.

## Main Recommendations

- Ensure Google can access your content as users do
- Use descriptive URLs organized logically
- Create original, helpful, well-written content
- Optimize images with alt text
- Build natural links with good anchor text
- Craft compelling title links and meta descriptions
- Promote your content authentically

## What NOT to Focus On

- Keyword stuffing
- Exact keyword domain names
- Specific word counts
- Treating E-E-A-T as a direct ranking factor

## Next Steps

- Set up Search Console
- Maintain your site long-term
- Use structured data for enhanced search features
