# Web Story Content Policies

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/web-stories-content-policy

## Overview

Web Stories must comply with multiple policy frameworks to appear on Google Discover and Search:

- **Discover policies**
- **Google Search Essentials**
- **Google Search's overall policies**
- **Search Feature content policies** (for richer experiences like carousels)

Sites with egregious violations may be permanently excluded from enhanced search experiences.

## Content Policy Requirements

### Copyrighted Content

Web Stories cannot include copyrighted material without permission. Creators bear responsibility for securing rights; Google doesn't obtain permissions on creators' behalf. Infringing content may be blocked from appearing.

### Text-Heavy Content

Stories with predominantly text-based pages are discouraged. Pages exceeding "180 characters of text" are flagged as ineligible. Short videos under 60 seconds per page are encouraged as alternatives.

### Low-Quality Assets

Images and videos cannot be stretched, pixelated, or otherwise degraded in ways that diminish viewing experience.

### Narrative Structure

Each story requires "a binding theme or narrative structure from page to page" - disconnected content is prohibited.

### Completeness

Stories must be self-contained; users shouldn't need to leave the story for essential information.

### Commercial Restrictions

Stories cannot exist solely to advertise products or services. Affiliate links are permitted if "restricted to a minor part" of the story. Display ads must follow AMP Story Ad Guidelines and comply with search spam policies.
