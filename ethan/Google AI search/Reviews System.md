# Google Search's Reviews System

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/reviews-system

Google's reviews system "aims to better reward high quality reviews, which is content that provides insightful analysis and original research and is written by experts or enthusiasts who know the topic well."

## Key Characteristics

The reviews system evaluates first-party standalone content created to provide recommendations, opinions, or analysis. It applies to various review types including:

- Single-item reviews
- Head-to-head comparisons
- Ranked recommendation lists
- Reviews across any topic (products, media, services, businesses)

It does **not** evaluate third-party user reviews on product pages.

## Current Scope

The system primarily operates on a page-level basis, though sites with substantial review content may receive site-wide evaluation. It currently supports these languages globally: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Russian, Dutch, Portuguese, and Polish.

## Recovery and Ranking

"Content impacted by the reviews system may recover over time, if you've made improvements to your content." However, the system represents just one of many ranking factors, so changes can occur for various reasons.
