# Write high quality reviews

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews

## Who Can Write Reviews

- Expert staff members or merchants comparing competing products
- Independent bloggers offering original opinions
- Editorial staff at news or publishing sites

## Best Practices for Quality Reviews

"Evaluate from a user's perspective" and "Demonstrate that you are knowledgeable about what you are reviewing - show you are an expert."

Key practices:

- Provide evidence (visuals, audio, links) demonstrating personal experience
- Share quantitative measurements across performance categories
- Explain competitive differentiation
- Discuss comparable alternatives and use cases
- Cover both benefits and drawbacks based on original research
- Focus on important decision-making factors
- Describe design choices and user impact
- Include helpful resource links
- Link to multiple sellers for reader choice
- Support "best" recommendations with first-hand evidence
- Ensure ranked lists contain sufficient standalone content

## Affiliate Links and Compensation

Reviews commonly use affiliate links - review Google's guidance on affiliate program policies.

## Key Takeaway

"Focus on the quality and originality of your reviews, not the length, following as many of the above best practices as you are able."
