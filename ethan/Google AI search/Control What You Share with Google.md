# Control the Content You Share on Search

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/control-what-you-share

This page explains how site owners can control what content appears in Google Search results.

## Main Reasons to Hide Content

1. **Restricting data** - Preventing sensitive information from being indexed while remaining accessible to on-site users
2. **Hiding low-quality content** - Excluding poor-quality or spam content that could harm site rankings
3. **Focusing crawler resources** - Directing Google's crawling toward important pages on large sites with duplicate content

## Methods to Block Content

- **Content removal** - Deleting content entirely from your site
- **Password protection** - Securing confidential material so only authorized users access it
- **`noindex` meta tag** - Using the robots meta tag to prevent indexing while allowing direct access
- **robots.txt rules** - Blocking Googlebot from crawling images and videos specifically
- **Opt-out options** - Excluding content from specific Google properties like Shopping or Hotels

## Removing Already-Indexed Content

Existing indexed content can be removed through Google's removal request process.
