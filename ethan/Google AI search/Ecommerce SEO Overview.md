# SEO Best Practices for Ecommerce Sites

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce

"A critical challenge for any ecommerce website is being discovered in Search." Sharing product data and site structure with Google helps shoppers find your site and products.

## Key Topics Covered

1. **Where ecommerce content can appear** - Understanding different Google surfaces where your content may display
2. **Share your product data with Google** - Selecting appropriate methods to communicate product information
3. **Structured data for ecommerce** - Using explicit markup to help Google understand and present your content properly
4. **Launching new ecommerce websites** - Strategic timing and registration considerations for new sites
5. **High-quality product reviews** - Writing reviews that assist customer decision-making
6. **URL structure design** - Avoiding ecommerce-specific crawling and URL-related problems
7. **Site structure and navigation** - Creating hierarchy and links that help Google identify important pages
8. **Pagination and page loading** - Understanding how user experience patterns affect Google's ability to crawl and index content

Many recommendations apply equally to sites listing brick-and-mortar-only products.
