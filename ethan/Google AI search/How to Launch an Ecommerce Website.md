# How to Launch a New Ecommerce Website

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/how-to-launch-an-ecommerce-website

## Core Launch Steps

1. "Verify your site ownership with Google"
2. Request indexing via URL inspection tool (small sites) or sitemaps (large sites)
3. Monitor indexing using the Page Indexing report
4. Establish business details for physical locations
5. Register with Google Merchant Center for product visibility

## Launch Approaches

**Grand Reveal**: Keep the site password-protected until launch, then follow all steps immediately. Pros include marketing surprise; cons include delayed search visibility.

**Home Page Launch**: Start with only a placeholder homepage accessible to Google. This registers your domain early but delays full product indexing.

**Launch Without Product Availability**: Release the complete site but mark products as out of stock initially. This allows content indexing while controlling purchase capability. The guide cautions against disabling add-to-cart functionality, as Google uses this "to verify the final price details."

**Soft Launch**: Enable the full site immediately upon production readiness. This simplifies implementation but risks unplanned social media exposure.

## Key Considerations

Google Merchant Center signup is essential for shopping tab visibility and rich features like product badges on Google Images.
