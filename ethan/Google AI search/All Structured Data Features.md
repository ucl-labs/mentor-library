# Structured Data Markup that Google Search Supports

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/search-gallery

Google uses structured data to understand the content on the page and show that content in a richer appearance in search results, which is called a rich result.

## Supported Features by Category

**Content Types:**
- Article (news, sports, blog articles)
- Video content with key moments
- Recipe with carousel options
- Event listings
- Course lists and educational content

**E-commerce Features:**
- Product information and pricing
- Review snippets
- Merchant listings and shipping policies
- Loyalty program details

**Organization and Business:**
- Local business information
- Organization details
- Employer aggregate ratings
- Job postings

**Other Notable Features:**
- Breadcrumb navigation markup
- FAQ pages
- Subscription/paywalled content indicators
- Vacation rental information
- Discussion forums and Q&A pages
