# Preventing and Monitoring Abuse on Your Site

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/security

## Overview

This Google Search Central documentation page addresses website security and abuse prevention. It covers multiple security topics to help site owners protect their websites and maintain good standing with Google Search.

## Topics Covered

### Prevent User-Generated Spam

Addresses how spammers exploit open comment forms and user-generated content inputs, offering prevention and monitoring strategies. Spammers often take advantage of open comment forms and other user-generated content inputs.

### Malware and Unwanted Software

Provides guidance on identifying malware, understanding distribution risks, and resolving related issues.

### Prevent Malware Infection

Offers practical tips and recommendations for protecting websites from malware threats.

### Social Engineering

Covers phishing and deceptive site practices with remediation guidance.

### Google Safe Browsing Repeat Offenders Policy

Explains how Safe Browsing protects users through warnings on dangerous sites and downloads. The policy addresses sites that repeatedly violate Safe Browsing policies.

## Key Information

The page explains that "spammers often take advantage of open comment forms and other user generated content inputs." This suggests site owners should implement protective measures for interactive features.

## Navigation Context

The page is part of Google Search Central's broader "Monitoring and debugging" section, which includes subsections on preventing and monitoring abuse, malware, and security-related topics.
