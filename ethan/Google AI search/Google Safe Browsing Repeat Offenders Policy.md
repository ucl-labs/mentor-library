# Google Safe Browsing Repeat Offenders Policy

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/security/safe-browsing-repeat-offenders

## Overview

Google Safe Browsing protects users by displaying warnings on dangerous sites and malicious downloads. It also alerts website owners about compromised sites, helping them resolve issues to keep visitors safer.

## Repeat Offender Classification

Sites that "repeatedly switch between compliant and noncompliant behavior within a short window of time" receive Repeat Offender designation.

## Key Consequences

When a site receives this classification:

- Website owners are notified via email at their registered Search Console address
- Owners **cannot** request additional reviews through Search Console
- The status persists for 30 days
- After 30 days, review requests become available again

## Summary

The policy aims to prevent sites from cycling between safe and unsafe states. Once flagged, sites face temporary restrictions on the review process, incentivizing sustained compliance with Google's safety standards.
