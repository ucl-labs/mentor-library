# Structured Data for Ecommerce Sites

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/include-structured-data-relevant-to-ecommerce

Structured data is "a standardized machine-readable format for providing information about a page" that helps Google better understand ecommerce website content.

## Key Structured Data Types for Ecommerce

**BreadcrumbList** helps communicate site hierarchy, potentially improving how breadcrumb trails appear in search results.

**LocalBusiness** is recommended for physical store locations, allowing you to specify details like opening hours and address. Also register with Google My Business and Google Merchant Center.

**Organization** markup lets retailers share company-level details including logos, contact information, and business-wide policies like returns.

**Product and ProductGroup** markup enables detailed product information sharing with Google, with options for variants. This connects to Merchant Center for shopping features.

**Review** structured data helps Google properly interpret and display product reviews in search results.

**VideoObject** markup assists with displaying video content - whether product demos or livestreams - appropriately in search results.

## Implementation Note

Ecommerce platforms using content management systems may offer integrated plugins to simplify adding structured data, rather than manual implementation.
