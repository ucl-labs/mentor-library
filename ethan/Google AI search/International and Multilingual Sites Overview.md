# Overview of International and Multilingual Site Topics

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international

## Main Content

This page introduces three core topics for international and multilingual SEO:

**Managing Multi-Regional and Multilingual Sites**: Explains how to optimize Google Search results when offering different content to users based on language, country, or region.

**Localized Version Declarations**: Website owners with multiple page versions for different languages or regions should communicate these variations to Google. This helps ensure users receive "the most appropriate version of your page by language or region."

**Locale-Adaptive Page Crawling**: Sites returning different content based on visitor location or language preferences may face challenges. Google "might not crawl, index, or rank all your content for different locales."
