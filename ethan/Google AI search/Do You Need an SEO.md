# Do you need an SEO?

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo

## What is an SEO?

SEO stands for "search engine optimization" or "search engine optimizer." Hiring an SEO "can potentially improve your site and save time," though it carries risks of reputational damage if done irresponsibly.

## Services SEOs Provide

Professional SEOs typically offer several valuable services:

- Website content and structural analysis
- Technical development guidance (hosting, redirects, JavaScript implementation)
- Content creation and strategy
- Keyword research and analysis
- Training in search optimization practices
- Industry and geographic expertise

## Getting Started Yourself

For small local businesses, Google recommends starting with free resources before hiring professionals. These include their video series on building online presence, Search Essentials documentation, and the SEO Starter Guide. Results typically take "four months to a year" to materialize after implementing changes.

## Choosing an SEO

Key considerations include:

- **Interview candidates** about their methodologies, experience, and measurable results
- **Check references** from previous clients
- **Request a technical audit** (you may need to pay for this)
- **Be skeptical of guarantees** - no legitimate SEO can promise top rankings

## Red Flags to Avoid

Google warns against SEOs who:

- Guarantee #1 rankings or claim special Google relationships
- Use deceptive practices like shadow domains or doorway pages
- Engage in link-buying schemes
- Send unsolicited spam emails
- Operate without transparency about their methods
