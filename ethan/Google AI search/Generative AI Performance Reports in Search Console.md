# Google - Search Generative AI Performance Reports in Search Console

**Source:** developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports (official Google Search Central blog) + help: support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139
**Staged by:** aeo-radar 2026-06-24 (GOSPEL). Reason: not in KB; first-party AI-search measurement.

## What changed (announced 2026-06-03)
- Search Console now has a dedicated **Generative AI performance report** showing your
  visibility inside Google's generative AI surfaces.
- Reports cover **impressions** (how often your URLs appeared in generative AI features)
  and **pages** (which URLs appeared) for **AI Overviews and AI Mode on Search**, plus
  **generative AI features in Discover**.
- A new **toggle** lets a site owner choose whether their content is shown in AI search
  features. Google states opting out is **not** used as a ranking signal for core web search.

## Rollout
- Began with a subset of **UK** site owners (June 2026); now **expanding beyond the UK**
  (US, Switzerland reported), global rollout planned, no firm date. Confirmed expanding by
  Google's John Mueller.

## Why it matters for us / clients
- This is the **first time Google itself measures AI-search visibility** - previously we
  could only infer it. Directly supports AEO reporting for clients (prove AI Overviews/AI
  Mode impressions).
- The opt-out toggle is a client decision point: appearing in AI features vs withholding
  content. Default recommendation: stay in (opting out doesn't help rankings and kills AI
  visibility).
