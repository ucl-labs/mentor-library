# Help Google Understand Your Ecommerce Website Structure

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/help-google-understand-your-ecommerce-site-structure

## Overview

Google analyzes website linkages and navigation structures to understand site hierarchy and page importance. The number of links needed to reach a page and inbound link count help Google infer relative importance.

## Key Recommendations

### Make Navigation Crawler-Friendly

- Create hierarchical links: menus → categories → subcategories → products
- Use `<a href>` tags for navigation rather than JavaScript events
- Ensure all products you want indexed are directly linkable

### Address Discoverability Gaps

"If category pages don't include direct links to all products in a category, Googlebot might not find all of your products by crawling alone." Use sitemaps or Google Merchant Center feeds for products not reachable through standard navigation.

### Highlight Important Content

Google prioritizes pages with more inbound links. Link to bestselling products from homepages, blog posts, and newsletters to signal importance.

## Core Principle

"The ultimate ecommerce SEO best practice is to create useful and interesting content that is valuable to users." Rather than optimizing URL structure alone, focus on meaningful internal linking that reflects genuine content value.
