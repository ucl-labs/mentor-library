# Establish Your Business Details with Google

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/establish-business-details

## Overview

This Google Search Central documentation page explains how businesses can enhance their online presence in Google Search results by establishing official business details.

## Main Sections

### Claim Your Local Business

Businesses can manage their Google Maps and Search appearance by claiming their Business Profile at business.google.com. Verification allows owners to provide addresses, contact information, business classifications, and photos for display in Maps and knowledge panels.

### Register Your Website with Search Console

The foundational step involves "verifying website ownership in Search Console" to establish official site presence. This verification process confirms site operators and enables monitoring of how Google displays site information.

### Update Your Site's Google Knowledge Panel

Google's algorithms automatically gather publicly available information like site names and social profiles. Verified representatives can "update your Google knowledge panel" to provide additional information or override automatically detected data for broader recognition.

### Add Structured Data

The page recommends implementing structured data to help Google understand page content. Recommended implementations include:

- **Site Logo**: Use Organization structured data to specify logo preferences
- **Breadcrumbs**: Implement Breadcrumb structured data to clarify site hierarchy

### Highlight Customer Support Methods

Businesses should follow best practices to ensure accurate customer contact information displays across Google properties.

## Troubleshooting

The guide suggests using the Rich Results Test tool to validate markup and recommends waiting approximately one week for Google to discover and update site details following changes.
