# A Guide to Google Search Ranking Systems

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ranking-systems-guide

Google employs numerous automated ranking systems to evaluate billions of web pages and deliver the most relevant search results.

## Core Concepts

The ranking systems operate at the page level, analyzing various signals and systems to determine individual page rankings. Site-wide signals also contribute to understanding, though strong site-wide performance doesn't guarantee all content ranks highly.

## Key Ranking Systems

**BERT** - "Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers" helps understand how word combinations express different meanings and user intent.

**Crisis Information Systems** - Dedicated systems surface hotlines and trusted content during personal crises, natural disasters, and emergencies.

**Deduplication Systems** - Eliminate unhelpful duplicate results and prevent featured snippet pages from appearing twice in results.

**Exact Match Domain System** - Prevents excessive ranking boost for domains designed to match specific queries exactly.

**Freshness Systems** - "Query deserves freshness" systems surface recent content when appropriate, such as reviews for newly released movies.

**Link Analysis and PageRank** - Systems evaluate how pages link to each other, with PageRank remaining part of core ranking systems since Google's launch.

**Local News Systems** - Identify and surface relevant local news sources through features like "Top stories."

**MUM** - Multitask Unified Model handles language understanding and generation for specific applications like vaccine information searches.

**Neural Matching** - AI system matching concept representations between queries and pages.

**Original Content Systems** - Promotes original reporting ahead of derivative content, supporting canonical markup for duplicated content.

**Removal-Based Demotion Systems** - Demotes sites with significant volumes of valid legal removals, personal information removals, doxxing content, or non-consensual imagery.

**Passage Ranking** - Identifies individual page sections to better assess relevance to searches.

**RankBrain** - AI system understanding word relationships and concepts to return relevant content even without exact keyword matches.

**Reliable Information Systems** - Multiple systems surface authoritative pages, demote low-quality content, and display content advisories for rapidly-changing topics.

**Reviews System** - Rewards high-quality reviews demonstrating expert knowledge and original research.

**Site Diversity System** - Generally limits top results to two pages from the same site, though exceptions exist for especially relevant searches.

**Spam Detection Systems** - Including SpamBrain, these identify content violating spam policies using constantly updated detection methods.

## Retired Systems

**Helpful Content System** - Originally announced in 2022, this evolved into core ranking systems by March 2024.

**Hummingbird** - Major 2013 ranking system improvement, later incorporated into evolved core systems.

**Panda System** - Launched 2011 to ensure quality and originality, integrated into core ranking systems in 2015.

**Penguin System** - 2012 system combating link spam, integrated into core systems in 2016.
