# Ask Google to Recrawl Your Website

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl

If you've recently added or modified pages, you can request re-indexing through several methods.

## Key Points

**Timeframe**: "Crawling can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks."

**Important caveat**: "Requesting a crawl does not guarantee that inclusion in search results will happen instantly or even at all."

## Two Primary Methods

**1. URL Inspection Tool (Individual URLs)**
- Best for requesting crawls of just a few specific URLs
- Requires owner or full user status in Search Console
- Note: There's a quota limit, and repeated requests for the same URL won't accelerate crawling

**2. Sitemap Submission (Multiple URLs)**
- Recommended for large numbers of URLs
- Particularly useful after site launches or major site moves
- Can include metadata for alternate languages, videos, images, and news content

## Monitoring Progress

Users can track indexing progress using either the Index Status report or the URL Inspection tool.

## Platform Note

Hosted platforms like Blogger or WordPress typically submit new content automatically - check your platform's support documentation first.
