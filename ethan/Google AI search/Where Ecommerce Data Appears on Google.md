# Ecommerce Product Data and Content on Google

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/where-ecommerce-data-can-appear-on-google

"Google has multiple surfaces where your ecommerce content can appear, such as in search results and on Google Maps."

## Where Ecommerce Content Can Appear

### Google Search
Product snippets and other structured data can appear in standard search results.

### Google Images
Product images can be indexed and displayed in Google Images search results.

### Google Lens
Products appear in Google Lens if data is uploaded to Google Merchant Center, product listings are enabled, and image best practices are followed.

### Google Shopping Tab
Products appear here when uploaded to Google Merchant Center.

### Business Profile
Businesses can claim and link their profiles to show business details in search results.

### Google Maps
Products with inventory location data appear in Maps search results via Google Merchant Center.

## Content Types for Customer Engagement

Beyond product data, retailers can create:

- Company background information
- Special seasonal offers
- Merchant and customer reviews
- Detailed product and category descriptions
- Educational content (workshops, courses)
- Live stream demonstrations
- Clear shipping and return policy information

The guidance emphasizes matching product descriptions to customer search terms and addressing different stages of the shopping journey.
