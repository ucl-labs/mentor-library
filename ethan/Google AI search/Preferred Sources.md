# Guide to Preferred Sources in Google Search for Web Publishers

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/preferred-sources

Website owners can help their audience discover their publication as a preferred source in Google Search. When users select a site as a preferred source, "your content is more likely to appear for them during relevant news queries in 'Top Stories'."

## Feature Availability

The preferred sources feature operates globally for queries triggering the "Top Stories" feature across all languages where Google Search is available. Only domain-level and subdomain-level sites qualify - for example, `https://www.example.com/` is eligible, but `https://www.example.com/blog` is not.

## Methods to Guide Users

**1. Social Media Deeplinks**
Publishers can share a URL format that directs users directly to their site in the source preferences tool:

`https://google.com/preferences/source?q=Your_Website's_URL`

**2. On-Site Buttons**
Sites may add a button alongside social CTAs using custom designs or Google-provided button assets. Google offers downloadable button badges in multiple languages.

## Additional Notes

These methods "are examples on how you can build your audience and help people find your site as a preferred source. It's not required to do them in order to appear as a preferred source."

Eligibility requires the site already appears in Google's source preferences tool; publishers should verify by searching their domain in that tool first.
