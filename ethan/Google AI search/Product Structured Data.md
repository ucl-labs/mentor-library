# Introduction to Product Structured Data on Google

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product

Adding structured data to product pages enables rich display options in search results, including Google Images and Google Lens. Users can see "price, availability, review ratings, shipping information, and more" directly in search results.

## Deciding Which Markup to Use

**Product Snippets** - suitable for pages where direct purchase isn't available, with enhanced review options including pros and cons.

**Merchant Listings** - designed for pages enabling customer purchases, offering detailed specifications like "apparel sizing, shipping details, and return policy information."

There is overlap between these features, so review both when selecting appropriate markup.

## Product Variant Support

Both product snippets and merchant listings support product variant structured data to help Google understand product variations.

## Recommended Additional Markup

Beyond individual products, Google recommends nested `Organization` markup for:
- Merchant return policies
- Loyalty programs

## How Shopping Experiences Appear

- Product snippets (text results with ratings and pricing)
- Popular products (visually rich product presentations)
- Shopping knowledge panels (detailed information with seller lists)
- Google Images (annotated product images)

## Result Enhancements

Ratings, pros/cons identification, shipping costs, availability data, price drops, and return information.

## Data Provision Methods

1. Product structured data on web pages
2. Google Merchant Center data feeds
3. Both methods combined (recommended for maximum eligibility)
