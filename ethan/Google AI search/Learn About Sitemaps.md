# What Is a Sitemap

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview

A sitemap is a file that provides information about pages, videos, and other files on a website, along with their relationships. Search engines like Google use sitemaps to crawl sites more efficiently.

## Key Functions

Sitemaps communicate which pages and files you consider important, plus valuable metadata. This includes:

- Last update dates
- Alternate language versions
- For videos: running time, rating, age-appropriateness
- For images: location within pages
- For news: article title and publication date

If you use platforms like WordPress, Wix, or Blogger, "your CMS has already made a sitemap available to search engines."

## Do You Need One?

**You might need a sitemap if:**
- Your site exceeds 500 pages
- Your site is new with few external links
- You have substantial media content or news pages

**You might not need one if:**
- Your site has 500 or fewer pages
- All important pages are discoverable through internal linking
- You lack significant media or news content

## Key Reminder

A sitemap can improve the crawling of larger or more complex sites, though it doesn't guarantee indexing of all listed items.
