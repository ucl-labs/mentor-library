# AEO Leader Frameworks - June 2026

**Staged by:** aeo-radar 2026-06-24 (DIRECTIONAL). Captured from leader LinkedIn/X posts.
Treat as frameworks/opinion to adopt, not platform gospel.

## 1. The "best [category] listicle" GEO hack is decaying - Lily Ray (Amsive)
- For ~2 years the easiest GEO play was: publish a "best [category]" listicle and rank
  yourself in it to get cited by AI. Lily Ray (2026-06-17, 629 reactions) says this is
  losing power as engines wise up.
- **Steal this:** stop relying on self-serving listicles; earn third-party citations instead.
- Source: linkedin.com/in/lily-ray-44755615

## 2. Engines trust third parties more than they trust you - Alex Halliday (AirOps)
- "AI search runs on citations and quality mentions. The engines trust third parties far
  more than they trust you." Also: "the deal can start without you" - buyers open with the
  category ("best enterprise X"), not your brand.
- **Steal this:** prioritise off-site mentions/citations (PR, Reddit, comparisons) over
  on-site claims. AEO = earned trust, not owned copy.
- Source: linkedin.com/in/alexhalliday

## 3. WebMCP - the cost of agent-unfriendly forms - Jason Barnard (Kalicube)
- Emerging concept: "A form without WebMCP costs an AI agent ~54 cents and ~131 seconds to
  fill. With WebMCP: far less." Points to agents needing machine-readable interaction layers.
- **Steal this:** watch WebMCP as agentic UX standard; agent-readability of forms/flows is
  the next frontier after content readability.
- Source: linkedin.com/in/jasonmbarnard  (verify WebMCP spec status before client use)

## 4. SEO and GEO are inseparable - Neil Patel
- "GEO without SEO means AI doesn't trust you. SEO without GEO means you're invisible."
- **Steal this:** sell AEO as additive to SEO, never a replacement.

## 5. AI Search Roadmap incoming - Aleyda Solis (Orainti)
- Launching "Crawling Mondays: The AI Search Roadmap Series" July 2026 - a structured AI
  search optimisation roadmap. Worth tracking for a vetted framework.
- Source: linkedin.com/in/aleyda
