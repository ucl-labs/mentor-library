# Shopify Catalog: Requirements for Inclusion (2026)

**Source:** Shopify Help Center (official)

To be **automatically included** in Shopify Catalog, your store and products must meet ALL of these:

## Store requirements
- Store complies with Shopify's Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.
- Store is on the **Starter plan or higher** and is **not in private mode** (password-protected).
- On an **Agentic plan**, products may be included through agentic storefronts before publishing to paid plans.

## Product requirements
- Products have a **title** and **at least 1 product image**.
- Products have a **price over $0** (free products are excluded).
- Products are **published** to the online store, Hydrogen, or Headless channels.
  - Hydrogen/Headless must use the correct route format to be included.
  - On the **Shopify Agentic plan** (no online store), products must include the **external product URL**.
- Products have an **identifiable product URL** (search engine listing).
- Products are **not Unlisted** and **not hidden from search engines**.
- Products **don't contain sensitive content** (e.g. mature content).

## Account standing requirements (new)
- Verified email and **two-step authentication** enabled.
- A track record of **genuine sales to real customers**.
- Orders **fulfilled promptly and accurately**.
- **Low chargeback rate**.
- Shopify invoices **paid on time**.
- Good standing with Shopify Payments (if used).

## No shipping-country gate
The official page no longer lists any shipping country, region or market requirement. Stores that only ship outside the US/Canada (e.g. NZ/AU) are still eligible for Catalog discovery.

**Separate limit - AI checkout, unverified:** third-party guides (Craftshift, Shugert, 2026) say built-in checkout inside ChatGPT / Gemini is only for US-based stores selling to US customers. Non-US stores are still discoverable, but buyers are redirected to the online store to pay. Not confirmed on Shopify's own help pages as of 2026-09-16.

## AEO takeaway / audit checklist
These are hard gates - a product failing any one is invisible to the agent catalog. Check during a Sprint: plan tier, private mode off, every PDP has title + image + price >$0, published + indexable (not Unlisted, not noindex), account standing (2FA on, verified email, low chargebacks, invoices current). On the Agentic plan, confirm the external product URL is set. For non-US stores, set expectations: discovery yes, in-chat checkout likely no.
