# Shopify AI Channels with Built-In Checkout (Agentic Storefronts) 2026

**Source:** Shopify Help Center (official)

Shopify's agentic storefronts for **Google AI Mode and Gemini** and **Microsoft Copilot** let customers complete purchases in the AI channel's Shopify-powered **direct checkout** without leaving the chat.

## Named AI channels
1. **Google AI Mode and Gemini** - United States only.
2. **Microsoft Copilot** - all eligible stores selling to US customers.

## How products reach each channel
- **Google AI Mode / Gemini:** via the **Google & YouTube sales channel** with product sync activated in the Product feed section.
- **Microsoft Copilot:** discovered via **Shopify Catalog**; merchants can remove access through the **Agentic** section of admin.

## Eligibility
- Store based in and selling to **United States** customers.
- Products eligible for **Shopify Catalog**.
- Agree to **Shopify Agentic Storefronts Supplemental Terms of Service**.
- Completed store policies: **Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Return/Refund Policy** (Settings → Policies).

## Setup steps
1. Shopify admin → **Sales channels → Agentic**.
2. Deactivate **"Allow Shopify to manage for me."**
3. Click the adjustment icon on the desired channel.
4. Activate/deactivate **"Shopify Catalog access"** and/or **"Direct checkout."**
5. **Save.**

## Checkout flow
Customer clicks **Buy** in the AI interface → enters shipping/payment in the Shopify-powered checkout → clicks **Pay now** (credit/debit card). In **Copilot**, customers can pay with **Shop Pay** (per the Editions launch).

## Unsupported products (won't sell via built-in checkout)
- Subscriptions
- Product bundles
- Digital products
- Customizable products
- B2B-only products

## Product data rule
Legal disclosures must appear **within the first 6,000 characters** of the product description.

## AEO takeaway
"Found" and "buyable" are different gates. This is the **Agentic Checkout / UCP readiness** layer: even a cited product loses the sale if the channel isn't enabled, policies are missing, or the product type is unsupported. Sprint/Premium check: policies complete, Agentic channel configured, product types supported, disclosures in first 6k chars.
