# Shopify Catalog: Overview (2026)

**Source:** Shopify Help Center (official)

Shopify Catalog is a comprehensive **global catalog of eligible products** sold by stores on Shopify. Shop, select AI platforms, shopping sites, and AI agents can search Shopify Catalog to display product information such as pricing, options, and availability in real time. It lets customers discover your products on those platforms.

## Automatic inclusion (no action needed)
- If your store and products meet the requirements, your products are **automatically included** in Shopify Catalog - you don't need to take any additional action.
- You **cannot opt out of Shopify Catalog itself.** You *can* stop individual agentic storefronts (AI channels) from accessing your products.
- Stopping a channel does **not** remove products from the Catalog - they remain available for other uses such as storefront search and Shop.

## Optimizing (the part a human controls)
- Provide **accurate, detailed, descriptive** product information: titles, descriptions, images, and other product-organization attributes, so AI platforms can recognize and display products effectively.
- If you use custom data or grouping logic, you can **map product data to preferred sources** and manage how products display (see [[shopify-catalog-mapping-2026]]).

## Data syndication on AI channels
- AI channels that access Shopify Catalog use your product data to power **discovery, ranking, and recommendations** within their experiences.
- With Shopify's agentic storefronts, products are made available through Shopify Catalog "with the depth and consistency that AI channels rely on."

## AEO takeaway
Listing is automatic; **ranking and eligibility are not.** The agency value is populating the fields AI ranks on and mapping data sources correctly - not "getting you into the catalog."
